HOW TO STUDY EACH LESSON

1. Read the mindset shift and problem before looking at the solution.
2. Redraw every architecture diagram without copying it.
3. Explain each tradeoff aloud in your own words.
4. Complete the practice tasks before moving to the next phase.
5. Revisit the case studies and replace one design decision with an alternative.
